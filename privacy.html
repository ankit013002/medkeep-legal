Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 September 2026

The short version

MedKeep keeps your medication list and history on your device. We do not run a server that stores your medicines, doses, or notes, and we do not sell or share that information with anyone.

What MedKeep stores, and where

Your medicines, schedules, dose history, refill counts, and notes are saved only on the phone or computer you use MedKeep on. They are not sent to us. If you delete the app, or use "Delete all data on this device" in Settings, that information is gone.

Reminder settings (snooze length, overdue window, refill alerts) are saved on your device the same way.

Exports (CSV or PDF) are files you create on purpose. Where you send them is up to you.

What leaves your device

Drug-name search and interaction information. When you type a medicine name, MedKeep asks two public U.S. government services for matching names and label text: the National Library of Medicine's RxNav service and the FDA's openFDA service. The request contains the name you typed (or the names on your list, for the interaction check) and your device's network address, as with any web request. These services publish their own privacy notices. We do not store the results anywhere except in your app's temporary memory.

Crash reports (optional, only if the build is configured with Sentry). A crash report contains technical details about the error and your device model and OS version. It never includes medication names, doses, or notes.

Usage analytics (optional, only if the build is configured with PostHog, and on the web only after you accept the cookie banner). We record events like "a medication was added" or "an export was created" with no medication names, doses, notes, or any health information attached.

What MedKeep never does

  • It never gives medical advice. It records what you log and reminds you of the schedule you set. Interaction information is a plain display of public label text and is not a recommendation.
  • It never shows ads and never sells or rents data.
  • It never requires an account in this version.

Notifications

Dose and refill reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Turning them off in Settings or in your phone's notification settings stops them.

Children

MedKeep is intended for adults and for caregivers of any age managing an adult's medicines. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds optional cloud backup or caregiver sharing, those features will be off by default, will ask for your consent before any information leaves your device, and this policy will be updated first.
