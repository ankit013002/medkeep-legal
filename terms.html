1. What MedKeep is

MedKeep is an app that helps you keep a personal list of your medicines, set reminders for when to take them, and log what you actually took. It also lets you look up public drug-label information for medicines already on your list.

MedKeep is not medical advice and not a medical device. It does not diagnose any condition, does not recommend a dose, and does not tell you whether to start, stop, or change a medicine. It only records what you tell it and reminds you of the schedule you set. Any drug information shown in the app comes from public government sources (see Section 5) and is for your own reference, not a recommendation. Always talk to your doctor or pharmacist about your medicines.

If you are having a medical emergency, call your local emergency number. MedKeep is not an emergency service and reminders are not a substitute for emergency care.

2. Reminders are not guaranteed

MedKeep schedules reminders using your phone's notification system. That system is controlled by your phone's operating system (iOS or Android), not by MedKeep, and it can be affected by things outside our control: your phone being off or out of battery, "do not disturb" or focus modes, notification permissions being turned off, software updates, or the app being force-closed by the operating system.

Do not rely on MedKeep as your only safeguard for taking a medicine that is critical to your health. Use a backup method, such as a pillbox, a second alarm, or a caregiver check-in, for anything where a missed dose would be serious. We will do our best to make reminders reliable, but we cannot promise a notification will always arrive on time or at all.

3. Your data belongs to you

In this version of MedKeep, your medicine list, schedules, dose history, and notes are stored only on your device. We do not operate a server that holds this information, and we cannot see it, change it, or recover it for you if your device is lost, reset, or the app is uninstalled. Back up your device through your phone's own backup system (such as iCloud or Google's account backup) if you want a copy elsewhere.

You can export your history as a CSV or PDF file at any time from within the app, and you can delete all of your data on the device at any time from Settings. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

Drug-name search and interaction information use two free, public U.S. government services: the National Library of Medicine's RxNav/RxNorm service and the FDA's openFDA service. MedKeep displays what these services publish; it does not edit their content for medical accuracy, does not guarantee it is complete, current, or error-free, and does not rank or score how serious an interaction might be. These sources can be out of date, incomplete, or temporarily unavailable, and MedKeep may fail to show a real interaction or show an irrelevant one. This information is never a substitute for asking a pharmacist or doctor.
